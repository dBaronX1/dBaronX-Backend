# DBX Token Prelaunch Compliance Checklist

## Public Sale Status

- No public DBX token sale until legal review is complete.
- No public sale page, checkout flow, countdown, allocation promise, or token purchase form before counsel approval.
- Token communications must stay preparation-only.

## Utility-Only Description

- Describe DBX only in terms of intended utility, access, rewards, or ecosystem participation where legally supportable.
- Avoid statements that imply equity, dividends, passive income, yield, guaranteed appreciation, or profit sharing.
- Do not promise profits, price increases, exchange listings, buybacks, or investment returns.

## Jurisdiction Review

Legal review must cover at minimum:

- UAE / Dubai VARA implications.
- Ghana entity, consumer, payment, and virtual asset implications.
- US user access and securities/commodities/consumer protection implications.
- EU user access, MiCA, consumer protection, privacy, and marketing implications.
- Geo-blocking or access restrictions required before any sale activity.

## KYC / AML / Sanctions

Before any sale:

- Select and integrate KYC/KYB where required.
- Implement AML and sanctions screening.
- Define prohibited countries, blocked persons, and risk escalation procedures.
- Keep auditable records for approvals, rejections, refunds, and suspicious activity handling.

## Litepaper Before Sale Page

A counsel-reviewed litepaper must exist before any public sale page. It should describe utility, token mechanics, risks, jurisdiction limits, team/entity details, and user responsibilities without profit promises.

## Legal Entity and Banking / Payment Readiness

Before launch:

- Confirm the legal issuing entity.
- Confirm banking and payment processor readiness.
- Confirm accounting and tax handling.
- Confirm custody, wallet, and smart-contract operational controls.
- Confirm support and refund policies.

## Private Strategic Funding Boundary

Private strategic funding may be explored only with counsel. Any private process must use appropriate documents, eligibility checks, transfer restrictions, and investor/customer communications approved by legal counsel.

## Engineering Boundary

Engineering may prepare internal architecture, documentation, and non-public prototypes, but must not ship public token sale code or activate a public sale flow until the checklist is cleared.
